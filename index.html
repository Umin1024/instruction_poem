1

Draw a line.
Draw it while eating.
Draw it while crossing the street.
Draw it while dancing.
Draw it while sleeping.
Draw it on your own body.

2

Draw a line.
Draw it while eating.
Draw it while eating rice.
Draw it while counting grains of rice.
Draw it while picking up grains of rice.
Draw it on each grain of rice.
Draw until you've covered a whole bowl of rice.

3

Imagine your finger is a grain of rice.
Place it on your body.
Throw it onto the ground.
Let it roll through dirty water.
Let it stick to the underside of a bridge.

A homeless man begs you, “Sir, please. I don't want money. I just want a bite to eat.”

Without a moment's hesitation, you flee in panic, unwilling to share any single thing that belongs to you.
